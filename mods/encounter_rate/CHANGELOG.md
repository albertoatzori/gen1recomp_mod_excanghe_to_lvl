# Changelog

All notable changes to this mod are documented here.
The format follows [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/),

## [1.0.0] - 2026-08-12

### Added

- Wild encounters can be thinned to HALF, QUARTER or OFF, through the
  `encounter.roll` seam on both Red and Gold. Fishing, scripted encounters
  and repel are deliberately untouched.
